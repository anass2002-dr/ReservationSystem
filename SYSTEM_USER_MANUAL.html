3. Reservations Schedule (Kanban Slots)

Visual time-slot dispatcher board with fluid drag-and-drop flight rescheduling.

/reservations (Schedule View)
Figure 3.1: Time-slot flight board organized into daily departure slots (08:30, 10:30, 13:00, 15:00, 17:00).

Operations on the Schedule Board

A
Date Navigation Pill
Use the < and > buttons or click the date input to inspect upcoming days or past historical flight manifests.
B
Drag & Drop Flight Reassignment
Click and drag any reservation card from one time-slot column to another (e.g., moving a customer from 08:30 to 10:30 due to cloud cover). The server updates slot timings immediately.
C
Print Slot Manifest PDF ()
Click Print PDF on any slot card header to instantly generate an official manifest list for mountain takeoff marshals and shuttle drivers.
D
Card Quick Action Buttons
Pay: Opens payment gateway modal | Print: Prints A5 / Thermal Voucher | Edit: Modifies booking details | Delete: Cancels reservation with confirmation.

6. Pilot Roster & Workload Management

Manage flight crew, track flights assigned vs flown, and ensure fair dispatch rotation.

/pilots
Figure 6.1: Pilot roster displaying flight quotas, active duty statuses, and pilot teams.

Workload Equity Rules

The system maintains live counters for each pilot:

  • Flights Assigned: Number of upcoming bookings allocated to the pilot today.
  • Flights Flown: Number of completed tandem flights landed successfully.
  • Equity Indicator: Dispatchers must check that daily flight assignments remain balanced across all pilots on duty.

10. Standard Operating Procedures (Daily SOPs)

Daily operational checklist for flight dispatchers and cashiers.

SOP Workflow
01
Morning Setup (07:30 AM)
Check exchange rates in header, verify weather forecast with takeoff marshals, and print the 08:30 & 10:30 Slot Manifests for shuttle drivers.
02
Passenger Arrival & Balance Settlement
Collect remaining balances (Rest to Pay) at cashier desk, toggle status to Confirmed / Paid, verify weight limits, and issue printed vouchers.
03
Evening Cash Reconciliation & Excel Export (18:30 PM)
Navigate to Reservations > List View, verify the Financial Summary table matches the cash drawer in TRY/USD/EUR/GBP, and click Export Excel for accounting records.
