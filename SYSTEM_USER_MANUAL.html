9. Multi-Currency Payment Ledger & Cashier Hub

Process customer balances, handle multi-currency payments, execute cross-currency conversions, print thermal POS vouchers, and audit refunds.

Operational Mission of the Payment Module

Operating commercial tandem paragliding in international tourist hotspots like Ölüdeniz / Babadağ requires extreme financial agility. Passengers arrive with varied foreign currencies (EUR, USD, GBP, Turkish Lira) and diverse tender types (Cash, POS Credit Cards, Bank Transfers). The Payment Module provides front-desk cashiers and flight dispatchers with a real-time settlement hub to collect pending flight balances, apply automatic or custom currency conversion rates, generate 80mm thermal POS customer vouchers, and safely process weather cancellation refunds.

Standard Operating Procedure: Collecting Passenger Balance

1
Locate Reservation Card in Takeoff Slot

On the Collect Payments tab, locate the flight slot (e.g. 08:30) and click Make Payment on the customer's card.

2
Select Customer's Currency Tender

Ask the customer which currency they wish to pay in. If paying in a foreign currency (e.g., Paying Euros for a USD price), select EUR from the dropdown.

3
Verify Dynamic Exchange Rate & Total

Verify the conversion rate (e.g. 1 USD = 0.8600 EUR). Click Pay All or input the nominal amount. The system will confirm: "129.00 EUR pays off 150.00 USD of reservation balance."

4
Process Transaction & Log Slip Code

Swipe POS card or count cash. Enter the POS terminal auth slip number into Notes.

5
Confirm Payment & Print 80mm Voucher

Click Confirm Payment. The card status instantly flips to solid green Paid. Go to Payment Records and click the Printer icon to print the 80mm thermal receipt.

9.3 Flight Cancellation & Refund Protocol

Strict Security Rules for Refund Execution
  • Status Prerequisite: The Issue Refund button appears only when the reservation status has been formally set to Cancelled (Status = 2) and the customer has collected payments (amountPaid > 0).
  • Amount Cap Protection: The system strictly caps the refund ceiling at the exact amountPaid. Frontline staff cannot issue a refund larger than the collected funds.
  • Negative Ledger Entry: Refunds are automatically recorded in the ledger as negative amounts (e.g. -50.00 USD) with an automatic [Refund] audit prefix in the notes.
