🚀 Turning Ground Operations Chaos into an Enterprise Software Solution:
How I Engineered & Deployed a Complete Paragliding ERP System in Babadağ, Fethiye.

When building real-world software, nothing beats solving a problem you have experienced firsthand with your own eyes and hands.

During my Master’s studies in Information Systems Management (MSI), I spent two months working on the ground with a commercial paragliding company in Babadağ, Fethiye (one of the world's premier tandem paragliding destinations). My role involved managing daily flight schedules, passenger bookings, pilot dispatches, and financial transactions.

What did I discover on the ground?
Despite high booking volume and premium ticket prices, operations were running in complete operational chaos:
❌ Relying on generic note-taking apps and messy digital sheets.
❌ Zero financial transparency: Unclear if a customer had paid in full, paid a deposit, or owed a balance; lack of multi-currency tracking (TRY, USD, EUR, GBP) and mixed payment methods (Cash, Card, Bank Transfer).
❌ Lack of accountability: If an error or double-booking occurred, there was no audit trail to identify who made the change or when.
❌ Pilot dispatch imbalances: No systematic tracking of who flew, who was queued, or whether flights were distributed fairly among pilot groups.
❌ Complete absence of business intelligence: No data on top tourist nationalities, peak demand time slots, highest-converting agencies, or top-selling flight packages.

Driven by my academic background and engineering passion, I decided to build a comprehensive, full-stack Enterprise Reservation & Operations Web Application from scratch to eliminate these bottlenecks.

✨ The Result:
A modern, 100% responsive, high-performance web platform built with:
🔹 Backend: .NET 9 Web API (C#) — Clean Architecture, EF Core, JWT Authentication, and Repository Pattern.
🔹 Frontend: Angular 19 (TypeScript) — Standalone Components, Vanilla CSS Design System, Leaflet Maps, and CDK Drag & Drop Kanban.
🔹 Database: MySQL / MariaDB — Relational Schema with optimized indexes and foreign key integrity.
🔹 DevOps & Cloud: Docker & Docker Compose with Nginx Reverse Proxy for automated deployment.

🎉 Commercial Milestone:
The application was successfully deployed into production and purchased by the company, where it now manages their daily operations, pilot schedules, and financial reconciliation in real time with continuous feature updates.

In this post and upcoming updates, I am excited to share a detailed overview of how this system works, how each module solves a critical business bottleneck, and the engineering decisions behind it.

================================================================================
PART 2: IN-DEPTH MODULE-BY-MODULE TECHNICAL BREAKDOWN
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. INTERACTIVE FLIGHT SCHEDULE & KANBAN DISPATCH DASHBOARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Route: /reservations (Schedule View)
• Purpose: Serves as the central mission-control room for flight dispatchers.

Key Features & Business Logic:
• Time-Slot Kanban Columns: Organizes bookings into distinct daily flight slots (e.g., 08:30, 10:30, 13:00, 15:00, 17:00).
• Drag-and-Drop Rescheduling: Powered by Angular CDK Drag & Drop, allowing dispatchers to drag bookings between slots in real time to adapt to weather windows and passenger arrivals.
• Instant Visual Status Badges:
  - Real-time payment indicators: "PAID IN FULL" (Green), "XX.XX DUE" (Red alert), "REFUND" (Danger).
  - Booking source & Agency identification (Direct vs. B2B Agency).
  - Passenger count (PAX) and assigned pilot names.
• Daily Manifest PDF Generator: One-click generation of official flight manifests formatted for ground crew, tower takeoff officials, and takeoff marshal checklists.
• 100% Responsive Adaptive Layout: Seamlessly transitions between multi-column desktop grids and touch-friendly mobile task cards.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HIGH-VELOCITY BOOKING & MULTI-PASSENGER FORM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Route: /reservations/add | /reservations/edit/:id
• Purpose: Streamlines customer onboarding, passenger details, and service configuration in under 60 seconds.

Key Features & Business Logic:
• Multi-PAX Group Booking: Allows adding single or multiple passengers under one primary reservation with individual customized packages.
• Smart Customer CRM Autocomplete: Instant auto-lookup for returning customers, auto-filling full name, phone number, date of birth, email, and nationality.
• Pilot & Group Allocation: Intelligent assignment filtering pilots by groups (Team A, Team B, Freelance) showing flight history and workload.
• Dynamic Package & Extra Services: Interactive selection of flight tiers (Standard, VIP, Sunset, Acro) and add-on services (4K GoPro Photos/Videos, 360° Cam, Merch).
• Real-time Balance Engine: Instant dynamic recalculation of total price, custom deposits, currency conversions, and outstanding balance as fields change.
• Safety & Compliance Check: Mandatory Passenger Weight Limit Verification switch before completing takeoff authorization.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. REAL-TIME MULTI-CURRENCY FINANCIAL & PAYMENT HUB
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Route: /payments | Top Header Currency Widget
• Purpose: Solves the multi-currency tourism revenue tracking nightmare.

Key Features & Business Logic:
• Multi-Currency Engine: Full support for TRY (Turkish Lira), USD (US Dollar), EUR (Euro), and GBP (British Pound).
• Dynamic Live Exchange Rates: Header currency widget with instant cross-currency converter and one-click rate synchronization.
• Multi-Method Payment Ledger: Tracks exact payment channels per transaction (Cash, Credit Card, Bank Wire, POS Terminal, Advance Deposit).
• Partial Payments & Deposit Tracking: Handles advance deposit collection at booking with automated calculation of due amounts upon arrival.
• Refund Management & Audit Trail: Secure processing of weather-cancellation refunds with historical ledger entries to prevent fraud.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. PILOT ROSTER, WORKLOAD EQUITY & FLIGHT LOGBOOK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Route: /pilots
• Purpose: Ensures transparent, fair, and accountable pilot flight distribution.

Key Features & Business Logic:
• Workload Equity Tracking: Live counter tracking "Flights Assigned" vs. "Flights Flown" to ensure equal earning opportunities across all pilots.
• Pilot Groups & Categorization: Organizes pilots by specialized shifts, licenses, and aircraft wings.
• Performance & Operational Metrics: Logs daily flight count, customer feedback ratings, and active/inactive duty statuses.
• Pilot Manifest Checklist: Enables pilots to review their assigned passengers, weight limits, and photo packages directly on mobile before ascent.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. CUSTOMER CRM & B2B AGENCY NETWORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Route: /customers | /agencies
• Purpose: Manages long-term customer relationships and travel agency partnerships.

Key Features & Business Logic:
• Comprehensive Customer Profiles: Complete demographic records, flight history, passport copies, nationality tags, and contact channels.
• Travel Agency (B2B) Portal: Tracks bookings referred by external tour operators, hotels, and travel agencies.
• Billet / Ticket Number Tracking: Reconciles external agency voucher numbers with internal flight records to avoid duplicate or fraudulent tickets.
• Commission & B2B Settlement: Tracks custom agency contractual pricing and agency balances.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. EXECUTIVE BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE & ANALYTICS DASHBOARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Route: /analytics | Dashboard Financial View
• Purpose: Transforms raw flight data into actionable strategic insights for business growth.

Key Features & Business Logic:
• Multi-Currency Revenue Summary: Comprehensive financial ledger breaking down Gross Revenue, Collected Cash, Card Receipts, and Outstanding Receivables by currency (TRY/USD/EUR/GBP).
• Nationality & Demographics Breakdown: Identifies top tourist origin countries (e.g., UK, Germany, China, Russia, Gulf) to optimize marketing campaigns.
• Booking Source Channel Attribution: Compares performance across Direct Walk-ins, Website Bookings, Travel Agencies, and Hotel Concierges.
• Flight Demand Heatmaps: Analyzes peak flight time slot preferences to optimize pilot staffing during thermal wind peaks (e.g., 10:30 vs. 15:00 slots).
• Excel / CSV Export: Complete one-click data export (`.xlsx`) for accounting, tax filings, and external audits.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. LOGISTICS, GEOLOCATION & HOTEL PICKUP INTEGRATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Route: Embedded in Reservation Engine
• Purpose: Eliminates lost drivers and missed hotel pickups across the resort area.

Key Features & Business Logic:
• OpenStreetMap / Leaflet Integration: Interactive in-browser map displaying exact pickup coordinates.
• Hotel Autocomplete & Geocoding Search: Real-time search for hotel names and locations in Fethiye, Ölüdeniz, and Hisarönü.
• Transport Shuttle Assignment: Assigns passengers to specific shuttle vans and drivers for seamless resort-to-mountain transfers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. MULTI-FORMAT PROFESSIONAL DOCUMENT PRINTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Purpose: Provides instantaneous paper trail for customers, pilots, and accounting.

Key Features & Business Logic:
• A5 Professional Customer Voucher: Formatted for laser/inkjet printers with corporate branding, terms & conditions, QR reference, and flight itinerary.
• 80mm Sewoo Thermal POS Receipt: Compact, ultra-fast thermal slip printing for fast-paced frontline cashier desks and takeoff gate passes.
• Digital Photo & Document Vault: Attach and preview passport scans, signed waivers, and payment receipts directly within each reservation card.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. SECURITY, AUDIT LOGGING & ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Architecture: ASP.NET Core Identity & JWT Bearer Token Security
• Purpose: Protects company financials and maintains strict operational accountability.

Key Features & Business Logic:
• Granular RBAC (Role-Based Access Control): Differentiates between Administrator (full financial access, rates, reports) and Flight Operator (dispatch and booking only).
• Complete Change Tracking: Every reservation record preserves immutable `CreatedAt` and `UpdatedAt` timestamps and user attribution to identify who created or updated any booking.
• Secure RESTful API: Protected endpoints with JWT Bearer authentication, encrypted password hashing, and CORS origin restriction.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. DEVOPS, CONTAINERIZATION & CLOUD DEPLOYMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Infrastructure: Docker, Docker Compose, Nginx, Alpine Linux
• Purpose: Provides zero-downtime, reproducible, and portable deployments.

Key Features & Architecture:
• Multi-Container Docker Architecture:
  - `reservation_backend`: .NET 9 ASP.NET Core Web API container.
  - `reservation_frontend`: Production Angular 19 bundle served via high-performance Nginx web server.
  - `reservation_db`: MariaDB 10.11 relational database container with persistent data volumes.
  - `reservation_phpmyadmin`: Secure database management GUI.
• Reverse Proxy & SPA Routing: Nginx configured for HTML5 browser history fallback, API proxying (`/api/*`), and static asset caching.
• Automated 1-Command Deployment: Complete deployment pipeline automated via `docker-compose up -d --build` with automatic healthchecks.
