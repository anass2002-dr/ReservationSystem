Paragliding Reservation System

Official User Guide & Operations Manual

Version 2.0

1. Introduction & Overview

The Paragliding Reservation & Operations Management System is an enterprise management platform designed for commercial tandem paragliding operations, flight dispatchers, cashiers, and booking agents.

  • Live Interactive Scheduling: Drag-and-drop daily flight slot dispatching.
  • Multi-Currency Engine: Real-time conversion & manual rates for USD, EUR, GBP, and TRY.
  • Cashier & Split Billing: Partial payments, multi-currency receipts, and 80mm thermal POS printing.
  • Pilot & Transport Logistics: Pilot attendance tracking (Flights Assigned vs. Flown) and vehicle shuttle dispatch.
  • Agency Voucher Integration: Manage agency partner contracts, billet numbers, and commission tracking.

2. System Access & Authentication

5. Reservations Module (Route: /reservations)

5.1 Schedule View (Kanban Slot Board)

The default operational view for daily flights. Each column represents a scheduled takeoff time slot (e.g., 08:30, 10:30, 13:00, 15:00, 17:00).

  • Drag-and-Drop Rescheduling: Simply drag any reservation card to another time slot column to reassign the flight hour instantly.
  • Print PDF Manifest: Click Print PDF on any slot column header to export a full passenger & pilot takeoff manifest.
  • Reservation Cards: Displays Passenger names, assigned Pilot, Total price, Deposit, and quick action buttons (Pay, Print Voucher, Edit, Delete).

5.2 List View & Financial Analytics

Switch to List View for date range filtering (Day, Week, Month, Year, All Time), search queries, Excel spreadsheet export, and currency financial breakdown.

6. Booking Wizard (Route: /reservations/add)

  1. Set PAX Count: Choose the number of passengers (1 to 20 PAX) to generate the passenger forms.
  2. General Booking Details: Enter reservation title, booking source (WhatsApp, Walk-in, Website, Phone, Agency), flight date, and time slot.
  3. Agency Bookings: Toggle Agency Booking to select the partner agency and input their ticket/billet number.
  4. Passenger Information: Search existing customer or type full name, date of birth, phone, email, and nationality.
  5. Pilot & Package Assignment: Assign pilot (with live flight metrics counter), select flight package, shuttle bus, and extra services (360 Video, Drone).
  6. Weight Limit Verification: Toggle verification switch for safety compliance.
  7. Pickup Geolocation: Select hotel pickup and search location with interactive map coordinates.
  8. Photo Upload: Drag & drop passport photos, ticket receipts, or waivers.

7. Payments & Cashier Management (Route: /payments)

7.1 Recording Payments & Split Billing

Click Make Payment on any reservation:

  • Displays Total Due, Deposit, Paid Amount, and Remaining Rest to Pay.
  • Supports multi-currency settlement (e.g. paying USD reservation in TRY or EUR) with automated live conversion.
  • Supports split payments (part Cash, part Credit Card / Bank Transfer).

7.2 Thermal Receipt Printing (80mm POS)

In the Payment Records tab, click the printer icon on any transaction to produce a standard 80mm thermal receipt with itemized flight details, deposits, and payment method.

7.3 Cancellations & Refunds

Cancelled flights with prior deposits allow issuing refunds with a safe maximum limit to prevent cashier accounting errors.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I reschedule a flight?
Open Reservations -> Schedule View and drag the reservation card to the new time slot column.
Q: Can a customer pay in a different currency?
Yes. In the payment cashier modal, select the desired currency (TRY, EUR, GBP, USD) and the system handles the conversion automatically.
Q: Where do I print the pilot flight manifest?
Click the red Print PDF button on any time slot column header in the Schedule View.
