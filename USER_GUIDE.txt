1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW & CORE FEATURES
================================================================================
The Paragliding Reservation Management System is an enterprise platform tailored
for tandem paragliding operators, flight dispatchers, cashiers, and booking staff.

Core Features:
- Real-time drag-and-drop daily flight slot dispatch board.
- Multi-currency support (TRY, USD, EUR, GBP) with live rates and manual override.
- Split payments, deposit tracking, multi-currency settlement, and refunds.
- Pilot attendance and flight quota tracking (Flights Assigned vs. Flown).
- 80mm thermal POS receipt printing and PDF flight takeoff manifests.
- Hotel pickup geolocation with interactive Leaflet mapping.
- Partner agency management with billet/ticket tracking.

2. ACCESS & LOGIN
================================================================================
1. Open your browser and navigate to: http://localhost:8080 (or your company domain)
2. Enter your assigned Username and Password.
3. Click "Sign In".
4. The system validates your credentials via JWT token and opens the Dashboard.

3. HEADER TOOLS & NAVIGATION
================================================================================
Located at the top of every screen:
- Live Currency Ticker: Displays real-time USD/TRY, USD/EUR, and USD/GBP rates.
- Update Rates (Admin): Manually set locked operational conversion rates.
- Quick Currency Converter: Instant calculator input to convert between TRY, USD,
  EUR, and GBP without navigating away from your active screen.

4. OPERATIONAL DASHBOARD (/dashboard)
================================================================================
- Total Reservations: Lifetime booking volume with growth indicators.
- Today's Flights: Total tandem flights scheduled for the current day.
- Total Revenue: Lifetime gross earnings tracked in USD.
- Active Customers: Total registered passengers in the directory.
- Today's Flight Schedule: Real-time table of today's slots with PAX count and
  status badges (Confirmed, Pending, Cancelled).
- Quick Actions: Shortcuts to New Reservation, Process Payment, and Rates.

5. RESERVATIONS MODULE (/reservations)
================================================================================
A. SCHEDULE VIEW (Kanban / Daily Board)
- Visual columns representing each active takeoff slot (08:30, 10:30, 13:00, etc.).
- Drag & Drop: Drag any reservation card to another slot column to reschedule
  the flight immediately.
- Print PDF Manifest: Click "Print PDF" on any slot header to produce the
  flight manifest for pilots and mountain marshals.
- Card Quick Actions: Pay (Cashier modal), Print Voucher, Edit, Delete.

B. LIST VIEW & ANALYTICS
- Tabular view with advanced filtering (Status, Flight Time, Date Range, Search).
- Excel Export: Click "Export Excel" to download complete filtered datasets (.xlsx).
- Financial Summary Table: Aggregated breakdown of Total Price, Paid Amount, and
  Remaining Balance per currency (TRY, USD, EUR, GBP).
- Channel Analytics: Passenger distribution by booking source.

6. BOOKING WIZARD (/reservations/add)
================================================================================
Step 1: Set Passenger Count (PAX)
- Select number of flyers (1 to 20 PAX) from the dropdown.

Step 2: General Information
- Title: Name of the booking (e.g., "Smith Family Flight").
- Source: Channel used (WhatsApp, Walk-in, Website, Phone, Agency).
- Date & Slot: Scheduled takeoff day and time slot.
- Booking Type: Direct Booking vs. Agency Booking.
  * If Agency: Select partner agency and input their Billet/Ticket number.
- Currency & Total: Preferred currency and total agreed price.
- Deposit: Enter upfront deposit and method (Cash, Card, Transfer).

Step 3: Passenger Information (Repeated for each PAX)
- Search Customer: Auto-fills existing customer details (Name, DOB, Phone, Country).
- Pilot Assignment: Filter by Pilot Group and assign pilot (viewing their stats).
- Package & Extras: Select flight package and multi-select add-ons (GoPro 360, Drone).
- Transport: Assign shuttle vehicle/driver.
- Weight Verification: Toggle "Passenger Weight Limit Verified" switch.

Step 4: Pickup & Logistics
- Select pickup service (Hotel Pickup, Meeting Point, Airport, Not Required).
- Type hotel name and search to view location pin on the interactive map.

Step 5: Photos & Attachments
- Drag and drop passport photos, ticket copies, or payment receipts.

Step 6: Save Booking
- Click "Complete Booking" to record the reservation.

================================================================================
7. PAYMENTS & CASHIER MANAGEMENT (/payments)
================================================================================
A. COLLECT PAYMENTS TAB
- Organizes today's flights into time slots.
- Highlights paid reservations, pending balances, or refund alerts.
- Click "Make Payment" to open cashier terminal.

B. MULTI-CURRENCY SETTLEMENT & SPLIT PAYMENTS
- Balance Summary: View Total, Deposit, Amount Paid, and Rest to Pay.
- Currency Conversion: If customer pays a USD reservation in EUR or TRY, select
  the payment currency and the system automatically calculates the conversion.
- Split Payments: Process part Cash and part Credit Card / Bank Transfer.

C. 80MM THERMAL RECEIPT PRINTING
- In "Payment Records" tab, click the printer icon on any transaction row.
- Opens an 80mm POS receipt layout formatted with itemized flight details,
  deposits, paid amount, and payment method.

D. CANCELLATIONS & REFUNDS
- Cancelled reservations display an "Issue Refund" button with built-in maximum
  refund safety checks.

================================================================================
9. QUICK FAQ
================================================================================
Q: How to reschedule a flight on the same day?
A: In Reservations -> Schedule View, drag the card into the new time slot column.

Q: How to print the pilot shuttle manifest?
A: In Reservations -> Schedule View, click "Print PDF" on the slot header.

Q: How to print a physical POS bill?
A: In Payments -> Payment Records, click the printer icon on any transaction.
